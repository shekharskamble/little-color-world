Privacy Policy

Little Color World · Effective 3 November 2026

In short

Little Color World collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect information. Your child's pictures stay on your iPad.

Who makes the app

Little Color World is made by Shekhar Kamble, an independent developer. You can reach me at shekharskamble@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or usage data, from children or from adults. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

What stays on the iPad

The pictures your child colors, and the app's settings, are saved only on your iPad so coloring can carry on later. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

When your child taps the camera and then the green tick, the app asks once for permission to add the picture to the Photos app. This is add-only access: the app cannot see, read or change any photos already on the iPad. You can change this at any time in Settings → Little Color World → Photos.

Internet use

The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet. If a future version adds optional downloads of new pictures, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and downloads will only ever happen when someone taps to check for them.

Children's privacy

Little Color World is made for children aged 2 to 5 and is listed in the App Store's Kids category. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. This is in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Links and grown-up areas

The only way out of the app is a button that opens the iPad's Settings, and it sits behind a grown-up gate: a short multiplication question that young children cannot answer. There are no links to websites, social media or other apps.

Apple

The app is bought and downloaded through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and may collect information under its own privacy policy, which you can read at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

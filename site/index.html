Questions parents ask

How do we save a picture?

Tap the pink camera button at the top of the picture, then tap the big green tick. The picture is saved to the Photos app on your iPad.

Saving says it can't reach Photos. What do I do?

Photos access was turned off. Open the Settings app, tap Little Color World, then Photos, and choose Add Photos Only. The app can only add pictures; it can never see the photos already on your iPad.

Will my child's pictures be lost if we close the app?

No. Every picture is saved on the iPad as your child colors, so they can carry on later. Deleting the app also deletes these pictures, so save favorites to Photos first.

Can my child undo a mistake?

Yes. The orange arrow undoes the last change and the purple arrow brings it back. The eraser clears paint from one part of the picture.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, so it is ready for car trips and flights.

Why does the app show a picture of an iPad turning?

Little Color World is made for an iPad held sideways. Turn the iPad to landscape and coloring carries on where it left off.

Which devices does it work on?

iPad with iPadOS 16.4 or later.
